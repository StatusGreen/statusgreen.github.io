Our mission is to empower clinicians with tools that are intuitive, compliant, and aligned with the realities of care delivery in Canada.

What We Do

We design, build, and implement tailored EMR enhancements that integrate seamlessly into Oracle Cerner PowerChart. Our solutions are not bolt-ons or external workarounds—they are embedded directly into clinician workflows to minimize disruption and maximize adoption.

Our consulting services include:

  • Custom PowerChart workflow design and optimization
  • Clinical documentation and decision-support tools
  • Canadian-specific regulatory and reporting adaptations
  • Configuration and extension of existing Cerner functionality
  • Clinician-centered design and usability optimization

Canadian-Focused Solutions

Much of the current EMR ecosystem is designed primarily for the American healthcare market, often leaving Canadian organizations to adapt tools that do not fully reflect local regulations, billing models, care pathways, or clinical practices.

We focus exclusively on closing this gap.

By grounding our solutions in Canadian healthcare standards and frontline clinician input, we help organizations move beyond “one-size-fits-all” implementations toward systems that truly support care delivery in Canada.

Our Approach

We take a clinician-first, system-aware approach to consulting:

  • We start by understanding real-world clinical workflows
  • We identify friction points created by generic or U.S.-focused solutions
  • We design targeted, embedded tools that reduce cognitive load and administrative burden
  • We collaborate closely with stakeholders to ensure sustainability and scalability

The result is practical, high-impact solutions that improve efficiency without compromising clinical autonomy.
