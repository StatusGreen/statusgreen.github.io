# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Referral and networking contacts: people who heard about Status Green Solutions through word of mouth or a partner introduction and land on the site to quickly confirm legitimacy and find a way to make contact, before reaching out. In practice this is IT/informatics leadership at hospital networks running Oracle Health (Cerner) PowerChart.

## Product Purpose

Status Green Solutions is a 3rd-party software consulting firm specializing in customized solutions embedded into Oracle Health Cerner PowerChart. The site exists to give a referred visitor fast credibility confirmation and a clear path to contact the company — not to persuade a cold audience.

## Positioning

Deep, narrow specialization in the Oracle Health Cerner PowerChart ecosystem — specifically custom PowerChart development using modern web based tools to provide user friendly interfaces to underlying patient data (specifically mPage development). A small, direct-access team already trusted inside major hospital networks (including Niagara Health, Waterloo Regional Health Network - WRHN, and Michael Garron Hospital - MGH). Status Green also provides custom CCL Report writing and development.

## Operating Context

Clients are hospital networks/health systems running Oracle Health (formerly Cerner) as their EHR. The work is delivered inside clinical environments: custom functionality embedded into clinician-facing PowerChart workflows, CRUD type workflows for underlying Oracle data through clinical friendly interfaces. Business is referral-driven (word of mouth, partner introductions) rather than paid acquisition or cold outreach.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed service types: custom PowerChart integrations; data reporting/interfacing. Other possible service types (e.g. SMART on FHIR/MPages third-party app embedding, workflow automation/clinical decision support) were not confirmed and should not be presented as offered until confirmed. Team size: 2. Time in business: 4 years. Full service list includes custom mpage development, CCL report writing and custom Discern Rules for logic based decisions. Key solutions are: Regimen Management mPage, Advanced Coder View, Patient Centred Appointements and PowerChart Search. Pricing is avaiable upon contact with Status Green.

## Evidence on Hand

Trusted by 3 hospital networks in Ontario (Niagara Health, WRHN and MGH). 4 years in business; team of 2. Successful implementation of intricate and customizable mPage for HIM Coders called Advanced Coder View at Niagara. Multiple implementations of the Regimen Management mPage, allowing clinicians to update Regiment Attributes, a functionality not present in the Oracle solution. Do not fabricate specifics beyond what is recorded above until that material is provided.

## Product Principles

1. Niche depth over breadth — the value proposition is deep Oracle Health Cerner PowerChart expertise, not general IT consulting.
2. Referral-credibility first — the primary visitor already arrives with a warm signal; the site's job is fast legitimacy confirmation, not cold persuasion.
3. Small-team trust as an asset — being a 2-person firm serving major hospital networks reads as agility and direct access, not a weakness to hide.
4. Healthcare-grade seriousness — a hospital IT audience expects a controlled, precise, non-hype tone consistent with regulated clinical environments.
